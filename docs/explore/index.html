OPEN QUESTIONS

What further work could change

Three routes into the existing question register. These are proposed next inquiries, unexecuted here; deeper work needs a separate selection.

Q-MEM-01 · UNRESOLVED EMPIRICAL QUESTION

What does a photograph help us recover?

Records can cue recall and support some checkable outcomes. The missing distinction is between recovering a memory and learning what the record shows.

A useful next output: a small evidence table with independent comparators and correct, error and omission counts. It could retain, narrow or withhold an accuracy claim.

Q-CA-01 · UNRESOLVED COMPARATIVE HYPOTHESIS

Does the wider frame change a decision?

Civilizational Alignment connects considerations across a wider frame. Its added value over strong capability, alignment or governance analysis remains unestablished.

A useful next output: compare the same case and sources under strong rival analyses. A consequential difference, or none, could change when the frame deserves use.

Q-UA-01 · FUTURE CONCEPTUAL CANDIDATE

Who belongs when new beings can be created?

Dignity, agency and plurality are commitments to examine. In a stipulated future with potentially conscious new beings, standing, voting power and material claims can diverge.

A useful next output: one case comparing institutional responses and who bears their costs. It could constrain proposed representation rules. This is not an active mission.
