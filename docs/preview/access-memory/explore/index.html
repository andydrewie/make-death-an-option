FROM ONE POINT OF VIEW TO MANY

Go Far Together.

We don't live in a lonely world. Other people carry pieces we don't have.

Imagine rebuilding an evening from several points of view. One person has a photograph from the stage. Another has a recording from the back. Through the music, a friend’s voice becomes audible.

One passage stays private. Its owner stays part of the work. The evening grows without anyone having to give everything away.

BEYOND KEEPING THE RECORD

What would you do
with another morning?

The ambition reaches beyond a better archive. Could continued life itself become a choice? More time to learn, change, love, build. Not only a representation that sounds like you. You, here for the next moment.

The right to continue belongs beside the right to stop. A longer future should leave room to become someone you could not have predicted.

And what might many different lives make possible together? New knowledge. Better medicine. More energy. Worlds to explore and things nobody has yet imagined.

Universal Altruism names the article’s farther ambition: expand what life and intelligence can become, while protecting their freedom to differ.

Indefinite lifespan and first-person continuity across substrates remain open research questions. This is a direction to pursue, not a capability already achieved.
