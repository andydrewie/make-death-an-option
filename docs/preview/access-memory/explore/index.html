What remains
to be worked out?

These questions point toward possible further work. They are unresolved inquiries, not findings or active research missions.

Q-MEM-01 · UNRESOLVED EMPIRICAL QUESTION

What does a photograph help us recover?

Records can cue recall and support some checkable outcomes. The missing distinction is between recovering a memory and learning what the record shows.

A useful next output: an evidence table with independent comparators and correct, error, and omission counts.

Q-CA-01 · UNRESOLVED COMPARATIVE HYPOTHESIS

Does the wider frame change a decision?

Civilizational Alignment connects considerations across a wider frame. Its added value over strong capability, alignment, or governance analysis remains unestablished.

A useful next output: compare the same case and sources under strong rival analyses, including the possibility of no consequential difference.

Q-UA-01 · FUTURE CONCEPTUAL CANDIDATE

Who belongs when new beings can be created?

In a stipulated future with potentially conscious new beings, standing, voting power, and material claims can diverge.

A useful next output: one case comparing institutional responses and who bears their costs.
