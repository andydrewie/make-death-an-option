# Research and the limits of each inference

Four bounded assessments support examination of the article. Their purpose is to identify what a claim can survive, including contrary evidence. They do not establish a complete theory of memory, aging, identity or civilizational coordination.

| Assessment | Strongest supported result | Boundary that remains open |
|---|---|---|
| [Memory fidelity](research/MEMORY.md) | Personal records can cue additional reported recall; specified procedures improve selected checkable outcomes. | General autobiographical accuracy, original experiential fidelity, identity and personal continuity do not follow. |
| [Biological continuity](research/BIOLOGY.md) | Finite human benefits, animal survival effects and selected repair, regeneration and replacement capabilities are demonstrated. | Repeatable integrated maintenance of all essential systems and an indefinite lifespan remain unestablished. |
| [Identity and digital selves](research/IDENTITY.md) | Records and models can support bounded representation, selected prediction, lineage and scoped delegation. | These achievements do not establish that the original experiencing subject continues. |
| [Civilizational Alignment and Universal Altruism](research/ALIGNMENT.md) | Existing research supplies component mechanisms, serious alternatives and institutional failure cases. | Moral truth, an automatic abundance-to-altruism chain and comparative superiority of the full framework are not demonstrated. |

## Read a claim at its own level

**Evidentiary assessment:** **Supported within scope** means that a stated population, procedure and outcome have relevant supporting evidence. It is not a portable guarantee. **Qualified** retains a claim with explicit restrictions. **Insufficient** means that the reviewed evidence cannot establish the claim. **Not assessed** marks a question outside the assessment.

**Claim framing:** **Normative** identifies an ethical or institutional commitment. **Conjectural** marks a possibility or proposed explanatory bridge that remains unvalidated. These labels are not rival evidence grades: a conjecture may have partial support, and a normative proposal may depend on empirical claims that require separate assessment.

Keep unresolved uncertainty, document publication, program readiness and operational authority separate from both. A published assessment can leave a claim unknown; a supported claim does not authorize an action; an adopted commitment does not establish that its proposed implementation works. Source-index access and locator labels describe the route to evidence, not its strength.

Insufficient evidence for a possibility is not evidence of permanent impossibility. A sincere commitment, by the same reasoning, does not supply missing empirical support. Distinctions remain attached to individual claims rather than compressed into one score for MDAO.

## Four tests of overreach

1. **More remembered detail is not automatically more accurate memory.** Photograph review can supply new information, prompt recollection or increase reporting. Independent comparators, error counts and denominators determine what an accuracy claim means. Reactivation paired with misleading information can increase both true and false endorsements. The [memory assessment](research/MEMORY.md) retains this favorable and unfavorable evidence together.
2. **A repaired part is not a maintained whole.** A function restored over a finite interval matters. It does not establish compatible repair across essential systems, indefinitely repeatable procedures, maintainable repair machinery or control of cumulative harms. The [biology assessment](research/BIOLOGY.md) treats burdens from supporting treatment as part of maintenance.
3. **A recognizable voice does not settle who exists or who may act.** Resemblance, commitment fidelity, a valid mandate, successor standing and first-person continuity require different warrants. The [identity assessment](research/IDENTITY.md) leaves favorable philosophical routes open without treating a credential as proof of personal survival.
4. **Greater capacity does not select a legitimate purpose.** Productivity can improve a measured outcome while benefits remain inaccessible or new harms emerge. Optionality can enable an independently chosen ethic; this differs from showing that abundance produces altruism. The [alignment assessment](research/ALIGNMENT.md) preserves those distinctions and the strongest rival frameworks.

## Evidence access and currency

These are derivatives of assessments completed in September 2026, not a continuously updated literature service. The memory and biology assessments use evidence available through 13 September 2026; the identity and alignment pages state their corresponding September scope. Review was targeted and predominantly English-language, with heterogeneous primary access. Some full texts, methods, source versions and null results remained inaccessible or only partly checked.

Source links identify the relevant public work; they do not imply fresh retrieval or a current correction/retraction audit. Reported statistical details remain subject to the access limits stated beside them. Several publications reuse a cohort, task, laboratory or control group. Publication count and repeated review of the same paper are not counts of independent replication.

The [source navigation index](SOURCES.md) provides a route back to cited material. The [objections](OBJECTIONS.md) identify changes that would matter. The [question register](QUESTIONS.md) converts selected remaining uncertainties into inspectable proposed work.

## Relationship to the article

The [article](ARTICLE.md) states the vision and its commitments. The [atlas](ATLAS.md) makes their structure explicit. Research may qualify or challenge an empirical bridge without silently changing either the published text or the status of a normative position. Future findings should identify the specific claim affected, preserve prior versions, and state whether the result changes an assessment, a research priority or a proposed practice.
