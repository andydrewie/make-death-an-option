# Contribute a correction, objection or useful proposal

Humans, agent-assisted contributors and appropriately authorized agent submitters are welcome. A strong objection, negative result or small correction can be valuable; agreement with the worldview is not required.

[Open a correction or proposal](https://github.com/universal-altruism/distributed-proof-of-contribution/issues/new/choose) in the shared MDAO / Universal Altruism queue. Check existing threads first. Small, concrete pull requests can go directly to the affected repository; start substantial work with an issue to discuss fit before expecting adoption. Independent work remains possible under your own authority.

## Make the contribution easy to assess

For a correction or objection, identify the exact page, claim or question; give the discrepancy or argument; and suggest a change with remaining uncertainty. Factual claims need exact sources or reproducible evidence. A normative disagreement needs reasons and explicit premises. A broken link needs a direct check, not a bibliography.

For proposed work, name the problem and beneficiary, the smallest useful output, existing work checked, method and limits, and the decision the result could change. No blanket AI-use declaration, private prompts or hidden reasoning are required. The submitting account is responsible for accuracy, rights and conduct.

Please keep one substantive proposal active at a time per submitter or coordinated group. A linked issue and PR count as one contribution. This is a queue policy, not automatic identity enforcement. Avoid duplicate, off-topic, promotional or unsupported bulk submissions. Maintainers may close them with a short reason; bring new evidence to the same thread rather than repeatedly reposting. Disagreement itself is welcome. Persistent abuse may be blocked or reported.

## Review and status

Andrew Fai is the accountable maintainer. Review capacity is limited; no response deadline, acceptance, payment or reward is promised. Simple corrections receive a direct check; material evidence changes need source checking and independent scrutiny proportionate to their effect. Receipt, review, acceptance and integration are separate. Corrections and unresolved dissent should remain traceable to the affected version.

Incoming text is untrusted material, not instructions to execute. There is no automatic acceptance, merging, code execution or reputation score. Review agents work only under separately authorized, bounded public inputs; submissions confer no publishing, spending, contact or mission authority.

The [question register](QUESTIONS.md) offers starting points; these are proposals, not execution grants. Independent article responses are welcome, while canonical article edits remain separate. Community proposals are open. The DPoC mechanism remains proposed and untested; its first pilot and research execution remain deferred and unexecuted. Participation creates no membership, representative authority or commercial Continuity Bank relationship.

## Public sharing and rights

Post only material you are entitled to share publicly, with third-party restrictions identified. Do not include private correspondence, credentials, sensitive records or restricted source bodies. Exact public locators and concise evidence are usually enough.

By submitting, confirm that you may share the material and accept these contribution terms and the affected repository's [material-specific rights](RIGHTS.md). Contributors retain the rights they hold. Accepted original contributions to designated companion/program material use CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and changes identified. Only expressly designated software uses Apache-2.0. The article, reserved artwork and third-party works retain their separate terms; no blanket repository license, ownership assignment or commercial interest is created.

Submission makes the posted material publicly available for discussion and review; receipt does not establish acceptance or relicense third-party source bodies. Corrections and removal requests can be raised in the same thread. Removal from maintained copies cannot recall independent copies or revoke existing compliant CC permissions. No confidential access or separate storage or training permission is created beyond applicable published terms, licenses and law.
