# Go Far Together

Return to the concert.

This reading follows the concert in [§12, “No One Saw the Whole World”](ARTICLE.md#12-no-one-saw-the-whole-world), and [§18, “Go Far Together”](ARTICLE.md#18-go-far-together). The excerpts below are Andrew Fai's published article wording. The concert is an imagined scene, not a recorded event or a demonstration of working reconstruction software. The accompanying abstract artwork is illustrative.

> Return to the concert. This time, imagine people trying to reconstruct it together. Someone who spent the evening near the stage wants to hear how the crowd sounded around their friends farther back.

## No One Saw the Whole World

> We don't live in a lonely world. Other people carry pieces we don't have.

From §12.

## Shared records

> Someone has a photograph taken near the stage. Someone else has a recording from the back, full of voices the people at the front never heard.

## A correction

> An agent helps align the shared records. A person who was there notices where the timing is wrong.

The uncertainty described in §12:

> One places a gesture before a change in the music. Another seems to place it afterward. Perhaps the timing is wrong. Perhaps the reports concern different moments. Perhaps there isn't enough evidence to decide.

> The system should allow room for error.

> For a moment, leave the seam visible. Don't smooth it away just because the composite would look more convincing without it.

## The plan changes

> The person who started the reconstruction wants to fill a gap. Their plan calls for a sealed recording, but its owner says no: that passage contains a private conversation. They want to contribute to the shared evening. They are keeping that part of it to themselves.

> So the group changes the plan. That passage will remain missing. They work on the part they can reconstruct from the material people have chosen to share.

## Their friend was singing

> When they play it back, the sound breaks off at the gap. Then it returns from the back of the crowd. The person who stood near the stage hears a voice they recognize rising through the music. Their friend was singing.

> The contributor who kept the conversation private is still there, helping place a photograph. Their refusal changed the work. It did not end their place in it.

## Adaptive Federation

> This is how I want us to build: able to disagree, correct the plan and keep contributing. **Adaptive Federation** is the name I prefer for that direction. Shared commitments, limited authority, many participants who can change the course of the undertaking.

> A civilization should be able to correct its founders without destroying the conditions that make correction legitimate.

> The scale can grow. The right to have a life of your own must survive it.
